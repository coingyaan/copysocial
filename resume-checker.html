What is an ATS Resume Checker?

An ATS Resume Checker is a tool that analyses your resume the same way a company's Applicant Tracking System (ATS) software does. Before a single recruiter reads your CV, it passes through ATS software that scans for keywords, formatting and structure. Our free tool replicates that process and gives you a score out of 100 so you know exactly where you stand.

How Our ATS Resume Checker Works

Our checker analyses five key areas that ATS systems evaluate when processing your resume. Here is what we look at:

1. Keyword Match

ATS systems are programmed to look for specific keywords that match the job description. Our tool checks whether your resume contains the most commonly required keywords for your target roles including job titles, skills, qualifications and industry-specific terms.

2. Formatting and Structure

Many resume designs that look great to humans cause ATS systems to misread or skip sections entirely. Our checker identifies formatting issues like tables, text boxes, headers and footers, unusual fonts and graphics that confuse ATS software.

3. Section Headers

ATS software looks for standard section names like Work Experience, Education and Skills. If your resume uses creative headers like My Journey or Core Competencies, some ATS systems will not categorise your content correctly.

4. File Format and Length

We check whether your resume is in a format that ATS systems can parse and whether the length is appropriate for your experience level.

5. Contact Information

A surprisingly common issue is missing or incorrectly formatted contact information. We verify that your name, email and phone number are easy for both ATS and recruiters to find.

Why Your ATS Score Matters in 2026

The job market has become increasingly competitive. In 2026, the average corporate job opening receives over 250 applications. Without ATS optimisation, even the most qualified candidates are filtered out before being seen.

  • A score of 80 or above: Your resume has a strong chance of passing ATS filters and reaching a recruiter
  • A score of 70 to 79: Your resume will pass some ATS systems but needs improvement for competitive roles
  • A score below 70: Your resume is likely to be rejected automatically by most ATS systems

Our free ATS Resume Checker gives you actionable feedback so you know exactly what to change to improve your score and your chances of getting shortlisted.

Pro tip: Always customise your resume for each job application. The keywords an ATS looks for come directly from the job description. A resume that scores 85 for one role might score 60 for another.
