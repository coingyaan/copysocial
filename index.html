FAQ
Common Questions
Is the ATS Resume Checker really free?

Yes, completely free. No account, no credit card, no daily limit. Upload your resume and get your ATS score instantly.

What is an ATS and why does it matter?

An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is software used by 97% of Fortune 500 companies to filter resumes before a human ever sees them. A poor ATS score means automatic rejection.

Does CopySocial store my resume?

No. Your resume is processed in real-time and never stored on our servers. Privacy is built in by design.

What's the difference between Resume Checker and Resume Optimizer?

The Resume Checker is free and tells you what's wrong. The Resume Optimizer (coming soon) automatically fixes those issues and tailors your resume for a specific job description.

Does the LinkedIn Post Copier work on mobile?

Yes. Just copy the post link from the LinkedIn app, paste it into CopySocial and extract the full text in under 10 seconds. No extension needed.

Which resume formats and countries do you support?

We support resume and CV formats from the US, UK, Europe, India, Africa, Asia and beyond. ATS standards vary by region and our checker accounts for this.
