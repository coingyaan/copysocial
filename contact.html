Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report a bug?
Email us at hello@copysocial.co with "BUG REPORT" in the subject line. Please include: the URL you tried to extract, what went wrong, and any error messages you saw.
Can I suggest a new feature?
Absolutely! Email us with "FEATURE REQUEST" in the subject and describe what you'd like to see. We review all suggestions and prioritize based on user demand.
Why isn't my extraction working?
First, make sure the post is public. If it's public and still not working, the post might be deleted or the platform changed their structure. Email us with the URL and we'll investigate.
Can I collaborate with CopySocial?
Yes! We're open to partnerships, integrations, and collaborations. Email us with "PARTNERSHIP" in the subject line and tell us about your idea.
How can I support CopySocial?
The best way to support us is to share CopySocial with others who might find it useful! Every share helps us reach more people.
