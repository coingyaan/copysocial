Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Here's how we protect it.

TL;DR: We don't collect, store, or sell your data. We use Google Analytics for basic traffic stats. That's it.

Introduction

CopySocial ("we", "our", or "us") operates copysocial.co (the "Website"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our service to extract text from LinkedIn posts and Twitter/X threads.

We believe in transparency and privacy. This policy is written in plain English because legal jargon shouldn't hide what we do with your data (which is almost nothing).

What Information We Collect

Information You Provide

Zero. We don't require accounts, logins, or any personal information to use CopySocial.

URLs You Extract

When you paste a LinkedIn or Twitter URL to extract text, we process that URL in real-time. We do not store these URLs. The extraction happens, you get the text, and we forget the URL immediately.

Extracted Content

The text content you extract is displayed to you in your browser. We do not store this content. It exists only in your browser session.

Automatically Collected Information

We use Google Analytics to understand how people use CopySocial. This collects pages visited, time on site, approximate location, browser type and device type. You can opt out via the Google Analytics Opt-out Add-on.

What We DON'T Collect

  • Your Social Media Credentials: We never ask for or store your LinkedIn or Twitter login information
  • Your Email Address: No account required means no email collection
  • Extracted URLs or Content: Not stored on our servers
  • IP Addresses: We don't log or store IP addresses

Third-Party Services

Google Analytics

For website traffic analysis. Read Google's Privacy Policy.

Cloudflare

For website hosting and DDoS protection. Read Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Your Rights

Under privacy laws like GDPR and CCPA, you have rights regarding your data. Since we don't collect or store personal information, most of these rights don't apply. To exercise any rights or ask questions, email us at hello@copysocial.co.

Contact Us

Email: hello@copysocial.co
Website: copysocial.co/contact

Summary: We built CopySocial to be as privacy-friendly as possible. No accounts, no stored data, no tracking beyond basic analytics.
