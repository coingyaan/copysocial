Since you can't copy text from the LinkedIn mobile app, the workaround is simple: copy the post link using LinkedIn's share button, then paste that link into CopySocial to extract the text instantly.

Method 2: Screenshot + OCR (iPhone, Android & Samsung)

Modern smartphones have built-in OCR (Optical Character Recognition) features that can read text from images.

iPhone (iOS 15+): Take a screenshot, open in Photos, tap the "Live Text" icon, select all text, tap "Copy".

Android with Google Photos: Take a screenshot, open in Google Photos, tap the Lens icon, choose "Text" tab, tap "Select All" and "Copy text".

Samsung Galaxy (OneUI 4.1+): Take a screenshot, open in Gallery, tap the "T" icon, select text, tap "Copy".

Method 3: Browser Developer Tools (Desktop Only)

For technically inclined users on desktop: right-click the LinkedIn post, select "Inspect", locate the post content in the HTML, double-click to select text and copy. Requires familiarity with HTML.
