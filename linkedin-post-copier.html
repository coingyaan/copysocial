Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about copying LinkedIn posts

How do I copy text from LinkedIn on mobile?

To copy LinkedIn post text on mobile, open the LinkedIn app, tap the three dots (•••) on any post, select "Copy link to post", then paste that URL into our LinkedIn post copier tool above. Click "Extract Post Text" and the full content will be extracted for you to copy and use anywhere.

Can I copy LinkedIn posts without a browser extension?

Yes! Our LinkedIn post copier works directly in your browser without any extensions or plugins. Simply paste the LinkedIn post URL and extract the text instantly. Works on all browsers including Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, Opera and all mobile browsers on iOS and Android.

Does this LinkedIn copier work on iOS and Android?

Yes, our LinkedIn post copier works perfectly on both iOS (iPhone, iPad) and Android devices. Copy the post link from your LinkedIn mobile app and paste it into our tool to extract the text. No app installation required - works directly in your mobile browser.

Is this LinkedIn post copier free to use?

Yes, completely free! No signup required, no credit card needed, no hidden fees and no subscription. Copy unlimited LinkedIn posts anytime. We built this tool for content creators, marketers and professionals who need to extract LinkedIn content quickly.

Can I copy LinkedIn articles and company posts?

Yes! Our LinkedIn post copier works with all types of LinkedIn content including personal posts, company posts, LinkedIn articles, LinkedIn Pulse articles, status updates, long-form posts, carousel posts and LinkedIn newsletters. Why can't I copy text directly from LinkedIn mobile app?

LinkedIn's mobile app disables text selection for most posts to protect content and prevent easy copying. Our tool provides a legal workaround by extracting the text from the post URL using LinkedIn's public API, allowing you to copy any LinkedIn post content for legitimate purposes like note-taking, research and content curation.

How do I copy LinkedIn posts on iPhone and Android?

On iPhone: Open LinkedIn app → Find the post → Tap ••• → "Copy link" → Paste into our tool. On Android: Open LinkedIn app → Find the post → Tap ••• → "Copy link to post" → Paste into our tool. Both work the same way - just copy the post URL and paste it above.

Can I copy multiple LinkedIn posts at once?

Currently, our tool extracts one LinkedIn post at a time to ensure accuracy and speed. For content creators who need to copy multiple posts, simply paste each URL one by one. We're working on a bulk LinkedIn post copier feature for Pro users.
